about

A student-run microfab
at the heart of CWRU.

Hacker Fab is a student-led fabrication initiative at Case Western Reserve University. We build and operate our own photolithography, thin-film, and vacuum systems using open-source tool designs developed at Carnegie Mellon and other Hacker Fab institutions worldwide.

Our goal: give undergraduates in MSE, ECE, Physics, ME, and CS access to semiconductor fabrication skills otherwise locked inside research groups. We build the tools ourselves, document everything openly, and run certified training workshops each semester.

We're not a course and not a research lab. We're a student-managed space where you get your hands on real microfabrication equipment, learn how it works, and contribute to an open-source body of knowledge used by fabs globally.

Our long-term technical objective is to fabricate a working MOSFET device — a multi-year project that gives every cohort a concrete, meaningful goal to work toward.

technical roadmap

Three phases toward
a complete MOSFET.

Each phase builds directly on the last. We move at student pace — deliberately, openly, with full documentation at every step.

01
active
Photolithography Infrastructure
Now — Pilot Semester

Establish a functional, safe, undergraduate-managed photolithography workflow using low-cost open-source tools. UV stepper already funded and under construction.

UV litho stepper build vacuum spin coater wet chemistry station EHS training SOP documentation MORE Center intro project
02
next
Thin Film Deposition Integration
Year 1–2

Integrate controlled thin-film capabilities to move from patterning alone toward full device-layer fabrication. Thermal evaporation, sputtering, and annealing.

thermal evaporator RF sputtering system tube furnace / annealing litho + thin film workflows simple device test structures
03
future
Full Device Fabrication & MOSFET
Year 2+

The long-term goal: a well-documented, student-fabricated MOSFET. A unifying multi-year capstone that ties together lithography, thin-film, etching, and measurement.

multi-layer mask alignment plasma / wet etching metal contact formation I–V characterization full open-source process docs
